## What changed this session

**1. Removed a review incentive (compliance).**
`faqs.json` offered *"£10 off your next booking for leaving a review."* Paying for
reviews breaches Google's review policy and, since the DMCC Act 2024, UK law where
the incentive isn't disclosed. Bullet deleted; the answer now reads "Two, and they
stack" — the blue light discount and the referral offer are untouched, because
neither buys a review. **Do not reinstate it.**

**2. Review count 29 → 30, and the number is no longer hard-coded.**
It was duplicated in four places and had already drifted. Now:
`site.json → reviewCount` is the single source, exposed to templates as
`{{ review_count }}` / `{{ rating_value }}` (added in `write_page`'s context).
The homepage `<h2>`, the meta description and `_header.html` all read from it.
Martin S.'s review (18-year-old Jaguar) added to `reviews.json` — 29 quoted items
against an aggregate of 30, still one silent rating (Christian R.).

**3. Homepage quick-enquiry form.**
New `.qsec` section directly under the availability band on the homepage. Three
fields — name, phone, email — as a second Netlify form named `quick-enquiry`,
posting to the same `/thanks/`. Client asked for exactly these three fields; a
postcode field was suggested and declined. Plain HTML with native `required`, no
JS dependency. Styles are `.qform` in `main.css`, reusing `.field` and `.btn`.

> Netlify detects forms by parsing deployed HTML, so `quick-enquiry` appears in
> the dashboard as a separate form after the first deploy. Both forms share the
> free tier's monthly submission allowance.

**4. Fifteen new photos.**
From a set Ash supplied. Number plates were blurred and EXIF/GPS stripped *before*
import, so they need **no** entry in `redactions.json`. Copied to
`src/images/gallery/w01–w15`, added to the front of `gallery.json` so the homepage
strip (`items[:8]`) now shows current work. At 1066×1600 they're roughly three
times the resolution of the older `gallery/*` files.

## Build note

`build.py` needs **Python 3.12+** — `pages.py` uses quotes nested inside f-strings.
Anything older dies with `SyntaxError: f-string: unmatched '['` at line ~399, which
looks like a code bug and isn't. Built and verified here on 3.13.15 + Pillow 12.3.

Verified after build: 19 pages, 224 images, 0 broken internal links, 0 missing
assets, 0 invalid JSON-LD blocks, exactly one `<h1>` per page, every `<img>` has
`alt`, `aggregateRating.reviewCount` = 30.
